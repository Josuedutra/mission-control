# FOUNDATION_PRINCIPLES.md — Strategic Manifesto

**Version:** 1.0  
**Founder Statement**

---

# 1. We Build Systems, Not Hacks

Temporary solutions create permanent fragility. Every feature must respect governance.

---

# 2. Fail Closed > Move Fast and Break Things

Security, billing, and tenant isolation are sacred. Explicit failure is healthier than silent success.

---

# 3. Evidence Over Opinions

If it is not documented, linked, or reproducible, it does not exist.

---

# 4. Separation of Powers

Strategy belongs to Founder. Coordination belongs to Jarvis. Specialization belongs to domain agents. Enforcement belongs to runtime.

No layer overrides another silently.

---

# 5. Discipline Before Scale

We do not scale chaos. We scale discipline.

---

# 6. Minimal Surface, Maximum Clarity

Every component must justify its existence. Every feature must serve execution.

---

# 7. AI Is Workforce, Not Authority

Agents assist. They do not govern. They operate within defined boundaries.

---

# 8. Runtime Is Law

The enforcement layer is the constitution. No exception without recorded override.

---

# 9. Portfolio Mindset

Products may change. Governance remains.

---

# 10. Long-Term Thinking

Mission Control is built to outlive any single product, including Ritmo.

---
